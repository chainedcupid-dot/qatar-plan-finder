Vodafone is required in terms of the Applicable Regulatory Framework to publish all the tariffs that have been notified to CRA on its website.


If you have any queries on any of the tariffs please contact our customer care department on 111, 800 7111 or contact us here. If your complaint is valid and remains unresolved for 30 days then you can also contact the Communications Regulatory Authority’s Consumer & Government Affairs Department on their 24-hour hotline number: 103; email: consumervoice@cra.gov.qa or visit their website www.cra.gov.qa
