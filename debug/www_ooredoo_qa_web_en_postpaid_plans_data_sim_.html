    What happens when I consume all my available data allowance?

    Not to worry! You can still enjoy using the internet at a throttled speed, depending on your plan. You can also restore your data to full speed at any time with our super convenient add-ons, which can be used to unlock additional data.
    Starting at just QR50, our great value Data Pack add-ons will provide an extra data allowance of between 4GB and 250GB to give you that extra boost when you need it most!

  • What is the difference between a Recurrent and a one-time activation pack?

    What is the difference between a Recurrent and a one-time activation pack?

    • Recurrent: The pack will auto renew, until termination.
    • One time: The pack will be active for 30 days.
