• Customers will be eligible for the Offer in case of a new connection, plan migration from Prepaid to Postpaid, port-in and
plan upgrade or moving from legacy plans to the mentioned plans.
• The Retail and Online promo discount (“Discount”) will be terminated if the plan is migrated from Postpaid to Prepaid, is
cancelled or if the Customer migrates the plan to another Postpaid plan.
• The Discounts are in addition to the current benefits in the above-mentioned plans.
• After the benefits expire, the Customer will automatically be reverted to the standard monthly rental.
• If the Customer terminates the connection, migrates to Prepaid or ports out within the minimum service period of three (3)
months, then the liability fees will be equal to standard plan rates.
